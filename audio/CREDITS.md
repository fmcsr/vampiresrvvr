# Audio Credits

All sound effects and music come from CC0 (public domain) releases on OpenGameArt; no
attribution is required, but the authors are listed here with thanks. Sound effects were
trimmed, converted to mono WAV and loudness-matched by `tools/import_sfx.py`.

## Sound effect packs

- [50 RPG sound effects](https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects) by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0
- [Interface Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds) by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0
- [RPG Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack) by artisticdude, CC0
- [80 CC0 creature SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx) by rubberduck, CC0
- [75 CC0 breaking / falling / hit sfx](https://opengameart.org/content/75-cc0-breaking-falling-hit-sfx) by rubberduck, CC0
- [100 CC0 SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx) by rubberduck, CC0

## Synthesized

Made for this project by `tools/synth_sfx.py` (same license as the project): `xp`, `level_up`, `core`, `gate_hum`, `heartbeat`, `fire_wave`.

## Music

- `dark_shrine_loop.ogg`: [Dark Shrine Loop](https://opengameart.org/node/12501) by qubodup (remix of yd's "Shrine"), CC0; plays in the camp
- `dungeon_ambience.ogg`: [Dungeon Ambience](https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-ambience) by yd, CC0; plays during runs
- `epic_boss_battle.ogg`: [Boss Battle Music (Epic Boss Battle)](https://opengameart.org/node/55649) by Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio), CC0; plays during boss fights and the final escape (converted to OGG)
